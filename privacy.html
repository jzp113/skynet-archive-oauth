Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10

Skynet Archive ("the app") is a personal backup tool operated by its sole owner for their own use.

Google user data the app accesses

The app requests access to the owner's Google Drive solely to create, list, read, and delete the backup archive files that the app itself uploads to a dedicated folder.

How the data is used

Files are uploaded as backups and downloaded only to verify integrity or to restore data. The app does not read, analyse, or modify any other content in Google Drive.

Sharing

No Google user data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party. Data is not used for advertising or to train AI/ML models.

Storage and protection

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's private server, readable only by the administrator account, and are transmitted only to Google over HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the stored token stops working.

Limited Use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Skynet Archive

Skynet Archive is a private, single-user backup tool. It uses rclone to upload compressed database archive files (Parquet) from the owner's own server to the owner's own Google Drive account, and to download them again for integrity checks.

It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is used only by its owner.
